The hardware panel. Every page here has a TECH button in the bottom right corner, or press T. It shows the frame rate, the delays between frames and the ratios between them, the memory this page is using, the draw calls and triangles, the GPU, the cores, the screen and the colours it carries. Every line says where its number came from — MEASURED here, READ from the browser, or CANNOT be known from a web page at all. The last one is deliberate: a browser cannot see DDR type or speed, and an invented figure would be worse than a gap.

Nothing is loaded from anywhere else. Not the 3D engine, not the fonts. Every page on this address was opened with every outside host blocked before it was put here, and none of them tried to reach one.
What is measured and what is modelled is written on each page rather than left for you to guess. On the routing scan the cities and distances are real and worked out on the page; which exchange connects to which is a model built from distance, because no carrier publishes its topology — and the page says so on its face.

No real player names, squads or kits anywhere. Those are licensed property. The shirts carry numbers.

Nothing here sends anything anywhere. There is no account, no tracking and no upload.
