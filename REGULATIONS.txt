Every competition below plays the SAME Laws of the Game, as
published by the IFAB. None of them writes its own Laws. What
differs between them is the competition regulations, and the
Laws hand those over explicitly: "competition rules must
state...".

So the engine holds one set of rules and a thin profile per
competition. Adding a competition adds a profile; it never
forks a rule.

The fields listed under each competition have NOT been filled
in, because that would mean typing a published regulation from
memory. Reading one of them returns the words NOT STATED, and
reading one through the strict wrapper throws. A field filled
in once is used everywhere that competition is played.
