FOOTBALL AI — PLAYABLE

Ten outfield players a side plus keepers, all articulated, all driven by the engine. You control the highlighted player; the switch is automatic when possession changes. Referee AI is live and now plays the Laws: offside with the flag decision, throw ins, corners, goal kicks, free kicks with a wall, penalties, advantage and dropped balls. The rules are a separate module with no renderer in it, so they survive a change of engine.
